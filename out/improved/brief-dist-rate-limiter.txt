PROBLEM STATEMENT
----------------------------------------
Multi-tenant APIs frequently experience 'noisy neighbor' spikes and DDoS vectors that degrade service for all tenants simultaneously. Traditional per-instance rate limiting fails in horizontally-scaled clusters because each node has no global state. Build a low-latency, sliding-window and token-bucket distributed rate limiting proxy that intercepts traffic and coordinates token consumption across a Redis cluster with zero race conditions.
