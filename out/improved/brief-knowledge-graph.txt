PROBLEM STATEMENT
----------------------------------------
Large document corpora (legal, medical, scientific) contain implicit relationships between entities that are difficult to query. Build a knowledge graph construction pipeline that automatically extracts named entities, relationships, and attributes from unstructured text, stores them in a graph database, and enables complex relational reasoning queries.

USE CASES IN THE REAL WORLD
----------------------------------------
Drug discovery (entity relationships between drugs, proteins, diseases), legal research (case law relationships), financial analysis (company executive relationships), and Wikipedia-style knowledge bases. Google Knowledge Graph and Bing's entity understanding are the largest implementations.

WHY THIS IS HARD (COMPETENCY REDUCER)
----------------------------------------
Knowledge graph construction combines NLP (Named Entity Recognition, Relation Extraction), graph database modeling, and reasoning — a multi-disciplinary ML engineering challenge. Engineers who can build knowledge graphs can work at the intersection of NLP, semantic web, and graph systems — a rare specialty.

RECRUITER SHORTLIST SIGNAL
----------------------------------------
Knowledge graphs are the backend of all major search engines and AI assistants. GraphRAG (Microsoft's graph-enhanced RAG) is the next evolution of enterprise AI. Companies building structured AI knowledge systems explicitly seek NLP + graph database engineers.

ARCHITECTURE & DATA FLOW
----------------------------------------
spaCy/BERT-based Named Entity Recognition extracting entities (persons, organizations, locations). Relation Extraction model identifying relationships between entity pairs. Neo4j Cypher for graph storage. Entity disambiguation using Wikipedia/Wikidata entity linking. Graph reasoning queries (shortest path, PageRank, pattern matching). React visualization using D3.js force graph.
