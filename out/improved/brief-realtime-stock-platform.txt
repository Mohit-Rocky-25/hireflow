ARCHITECTURE & DATA FLOW
----------------------------------------
WebSocket data ingester consuming market tick feeds (NSE/BSE mock data). Kafka decouples ingestion from processing. TimescaleDB for tick-level time-series storage with OHLCV computation. Redis stores active alert rules and evaluates them in-memory. React dashboard receives pushes via Socket.IO.
