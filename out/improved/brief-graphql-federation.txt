ARCHITECTURE & DATA FLOW
----------------------------------------
Apollo Federation gateway routing queries to 3 subgraph services (Users, Products, Orders). Each subgraph owns its schema fragment and resolves its entity types. Supergraph schema composed automatically by Apollo Rover. DataLoader for batching N+1 database queries. Persisted queries for production performance.
