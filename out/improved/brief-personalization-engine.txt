ARCHITECTURE & DATA FLOW
----------------------------------------
Kafka stream of behavioral events (clicks, impressions, purchases) → Online feature aggregator updating Redis per-user counters in sub-second. Offline embedding model computing user/item representations daily. sub-second scoring combining online features with offline embeddings. FastAPI serving with Redis caching. Bandit algorithm for exploration-exploitation.
