# Open-Source Fellowships & Research Internships Roadmap

## Overview
Open-source contribution programs generate **public, cryptographically signed, peer-reviewed commit histories**. For students targeting Tier S product companies (Google, Microsoft, Stripe, Uber) or top research labs, open-source maintainer endorsements carry higher recruiter weight than personal portfolio projects.

---

## 1. Code for GovTech (C4GT) India Fellowship
- **Organizers**: Open-Source Community & eGov Foundation
- **Stipend**: ₹1,00,000 INR
- **Duration**: 8 to 10 weeks (Summer)
- **Domain**: India Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) & Digital Public Goods (DPGs)
- **Tech Stack**: Java, Spring Boot, Python, TypeScript, Docker, Kubernetes, Beckn Protocol
- **Contribution Model**: Contributors build on production repositories powering national-scale citizen services. Mentors are Chief Technology Architects.

---

## 2. Google Summer of Code (GSoC)
- **Stipend**: ₹1,65,000 to ₹2,50,000 INR (Adjusted for Purchasing Power Parity)
- **Duration**: 12 to 22 weeks
- **Eligibility**: Open to contributors 18+ new to open-source organizations.
- **Winning Formula**:
  1. Pick 1 organization 2 months prior to proposal deadlines.
  2. Engage in public mailing lists/Discord/Slack.
  3. Submit 2-3 merged pull requests addressing `good-first-issue` bugs before proposals open.
  4. Write a milestone-driven technical proposal with architectural risk mitigations.

---

## 3. IIT Summer Research Fellowships (IITB, IITD, IITM, IITK SURGE)
- **Stipend**: ₹10,000/month + on-campus laboratory access
- **Duration**: 8 weeks (May to July)
- **Focus**: High-performance computing, deep learning, computer vision, robotics, distributed algorithms.
- **Outcome**: Peer-reviewed conference papers (IEEE, ACM, CVPR) or verifiable research prototypes.

---

## 4. Linux Foundation Mentorship (LFX)
- **Stipend**: ~$3,000 to $6,000 USD (approx. ₹2.5 Lakhs to ₹5.0 Lakhs INR)
- **Cycles**: Spring, Summer, and Fall (three cohorts annually)
- **Repositories**: CNCF projects (Kubernetes, Envoy, Prometheus, containerd, OpenTelemetry).
