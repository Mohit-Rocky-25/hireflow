# Pathways: Statutory Tax & Labour Rules Reference Guide

This reference document explains every tax, provident fund, and labour law rule implemented in the Pathways Offer Engine, along with official government citations.

---

## 1. New Personal Income Tax Regime (Finance Act)
- **Primary Source:** Ministry of Finance, Government of India / Income Tax Department  
- **Official URL:** `https://incometaxindia.gov.in/rules/tax-rates-new-regime`
- **Applicable Financial Year:** FY 2026-27 (Assessment Year 2027-28)
- **Default Tax Regime:** Under Section 115BAC, the New Tax Regime is the default regime for all individual taxpayers.

### Statutory Slabs:
| Taxable Income Slab (INR) | Statutory Tax Rate | Margin Tax in Slab | Cumulative Tax at Slab Upper Bound |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ₹0 to ₹4,00,000 | Nil (0%) | ₹0 | ₹0 |
| ₹4,00,001 to ₹8,00,000 | 5% | ₹20,000 | ₹20,000 |
| ₹8,00,001 to ₹12,00,000 | 10% | ₹40,000 | ₹60,000 |
| ₹12,00,001 to ₹16,00,000 | 15% | ₹60,000 | ₹1,20,000 |
| ₹16,00,001 to ₹20,00,000 | 20% | ₹80,000 | ₹2,00,000 |
| ₹20,00,001 to ₹24,00,000 | 25% | ₹1,00,000 | ₹3,00,000 |
| Above ₹24,00,000 | 30% | Dynamic | — |

### Key Statutory Deductions & Rebates:
1. **Standard Deduction (Section 16(ia)):** Flat ₹75,000 deduction automatically subtracted from gross salary before computing taxable income.
2. **Rebate Under Section 87A:** A resident individual whose taxable income does not exceed ₹12,00,000 is entitled to a rebate equal to 100% of the income tax payable (up to ₹60,000 maximum rebate). Consequently, **zero income tax** is payable on taxable income up to ₹12,00,000 (equivalent to ₹12,75,000 gross salary after standard deduction).
3. **Health and Education Cess:** 4% levied on the total income tax payable after rebate.

## 2. Employees' Provident Fund (EPFO Rules)
- **Primary Source:** Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952 / EPFO Circulars  
- **Official URL:** `https://epfindia.gov.in/circulars/statutory-contributions`
- **Statutory Employee Contribution Rate:** 12% of basic wage plus dearness allowance.
- **Mandatory Statutory Wage Ceiling:** ₹15,000 per month (₹1,80,000 annually).
  - For basic wages above ₹15,000/mo, mandatory employee deduction is capped at **₹1,800/month** unless joint declaration is submitted under Para 26(6).

---

## 3. Gratuity Statutory Entitlement
- **Primary Source:** Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 (Act No. 39 of 1972) / Ministry of Labour and Employment  
- **Official URL:** `https://labour.gov.in/gazette/code-on-wages-gratuity`
- **Statutory Formula:** $\text{Gratuity} = \frac{15 \times \text{Last Drawn Basic Wage} \times \text{Completed Years}}{26}$
- **Vesting Rule:** Mandatory minimum of **5 continuous years of service** with the same employer (Section 4(1)). CTC inclusions of gratuity for freshers do not confer immediate or short-term cash benefit.

---

## 4. Code on Wages (50% Wages Rule)
- **Primary Source:** Code on Wages, 2019 / Gazette of India Notification  
- **Official URL:** `https://labour.gov.in/gazette/code-on-wages-gratuity`
- **Statutory Requirement:** Basic salary, dearness allowance, and retaining allowance must comprise **at least 50%** of total remuneration. If allowances exceed 50%, the excess is statutorily treated as wages for PF and gratuity calculations.
