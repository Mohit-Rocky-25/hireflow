# Pathways: Student Terms of Use & Privacy Disclosures

**Effective Date**: October 26, 2026  
**Governing Principles**: Constitution Parts B & D, Spec C14  

---

## 1. Zero Runtime Network Egress & Privacy Guarantee

1. **Browser-Local Storage**: All profile information (academic branch, passing batch year, CGPA, Class X/XII marks, backlog counts), offer terms (fixed pay, variable pay, joining bonus, service bond conditions), and custom scenario configurations are evaluated and stored strictly inside your browser's local storage (`hireflow:v4:pathways:profile`).
2. **Zero Network Transmission**: When you interact with Pathways, no data is sent to HireFlow servers, third-party analytics trackers, advertisement networks, or external telemetry systems. Pathways functions completely offline.
3. **Data Deletion**: You can instantly wipe all stored Pathways data at any time directly through the "Clear My Data" button in the Profile Budget Bar, or by clearing your browser's local storage.

---

## 2. Computational Nature & Financial/Legal Disclaimers

1. **Computational Simulations, Not Advice**: Pathways outputs, including monthly in-hand estimates, partial-year income tax projections, first-year cash decompositions, and employer benefit allocations, are algorithmic calculations based strictly on:
   - The specific numbers and options you enter.
   - Statutory notifications from official government gazettes (e.g. Income Tax New Regime FY 2026-27 under the Finance Act, EPFO statutory contribution ceiling circulars).
   - These projections do **not** constitute legal advice, chartered accountancy services, or tax filing guidance. Individual taxation scenarios (such as specific section exemptions or multi-employer Form 16 reconciliations) require consultation with a qualified professional.
2. **Deterministic Eligibility Evaluation**: The Eligibility Radar matches your recorded profile against published cutoffs from official campus notices. It indicates whether you meet stated criteria; it does not guarantee selection, interview shortlisting, or campus placement.

---

## 3. Service Bonds & Liquidated Damages Disclosure

1. **Indian Legal Context**: Under Indian contract jurisprudence and Section 74 of the Indian Contract Act, courts have held reasonable service agreements and training bonds enforceable as pre-estimated liquidated damages, provided the employer incurred verifiable training expenditure.
2. **Money at Stake**: The Exit Exposure Timeline in Pathways illustrates the contractual financial exposure if a student separates before bond or clawback maturity. Whether a specific contract clause is legally enforceable in your jurisdiction is a legal determination. Pathways models the money at stake, not legal validity.

---

## 4. Employer Non-Affiliation

1. **Independent Evaluation**: HireFlow Pathways is developed and maintained independently.
2. **Trademarks & Sourcing**: All company trademarks, recruitment program titles, and logos referenced within Pathways are the intellectual property of their respective holders. Sourced criteria are retrieved from official public notices. Inclusion in Pathways does not imply any sponsorship, official partnership, or endorsement by the hiring organization.

---

## 5. Data Accuracy & Open Correction Policy

1. **Audit Trail**: Every campus criterion and compensation parameter recorded in Pathways is backed by an exact verbatim page snippet ($\le 15$ words), verified retrieval date, and official source URL stored in our open audit ledger (`docs/pathways/ledger/`).
2. **Corrections Workflow**: If you notice an updated cutoff, revised bond term, or changed platform for any campus program, you can report it using the in-app "Report an Error" feature or by filing a correction request via `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/pathways-correction.yml`. All candidate rows are reviewed and verified before inclusion in production builds.
