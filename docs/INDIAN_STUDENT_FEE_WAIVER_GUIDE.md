# Indian College Student Fee Waiver & Financial Aid Playbook

## Purpose
This playbook details practical methods for Indian engineering students to access global industry certifications at zero cost or with deep collegiate subsidies.

---

## 1. Coursera Professional Certificates (100% Free)
### Applicable Credentials:
- Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate
- Meta Front-End Developer Professional Certificate
- Meta Back-End Developer Professional Certificate
- IBM Full Stack Software Developer Certificate
- IBM AI Engineering Professional Certificate
- Google UX Design Certificate

### How Indian Students Get It 100% Free:
1. Locate the **"Financial Aid Available"** hyperlink next to the "Enroll" button on Coursera.
2. Select **College Student / Unemployed** as your employment status and enter annual income as `₹0`.
3. In the application statement, outline your engineering semester curriculum and career objectives.
4. Coursera automatically approves within **16 days**. Once approved, all assignments, graded labs, and Credly badges are 100% free.

---

## 2. Microsoft Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900 / AI-900) Free Routes
1. **Microsoft Learn Student Ambassadors (MLSA)**: Connect with your campus MLSA representative or join the global MLSA Discord to receive 100% exam vouchers.
2. **Microsoft Virtual Training Days**: Register for free official Microsoft Virtual Training webinars; participants frequently receive discounted or free examination vouchers.
3. **Pearson VUE Student Verification**: Verify your college ID via SheerID on Pearson VUE to unlock India-specific student pricing (~$55 USD / ₹4,800 INR).

---

## 3. AWS Certifications Discount Strategy
1. **AWS Educate**: Register with your college `.edu` or student email to access free hands-on AWS console sandboxes without needing a credit card.
2. **50% Exam Recurrence Voucher**: Once you pass any AWS exam (e.g., Cloud Practitioner), your AWS Certification Benefits portal provides an automatic 50% discount voucher for your next associate exam (reducing the ₹12,500 fee to ₹6,250).

---

## 4. CompTIA Academic Marketplace India
1. Standard retail price for CompTIA Security+ is $404 USD (~₹33,500 INR).
2. Visit **academic-store.comptia.org** and verify your Indian college student status.
3. The voucher price drops to ~$260 USD / ₹18,500 INR, yielding a **45% direct student saving**.

---

## 5. Tableau for Students (100% Free)
- Standard Tableau Desktop licenses cost $840 USD/year.
- Indian college students with a valid college ID card or Bonafide certificate receive a **1-year Tableau Desktop license 100% FREE** via the Tableau for Students program.
