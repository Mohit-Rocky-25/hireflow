# Indian Collegiate Hackathon & Codathon PPO Playbook

## Executive Summary
Engineering recruitment in Tier 1/2/3 colleges in India is increasingly shifting from traditional on-campus mass hiring to **open national competitive hackathons and coding challenges**. Winning or reaching the grand finale rounds of these competitions provides direct interview shortlists (PPIs) and Pre-Placement Offers (PPOs) that bypass resume screening ATS filters.

---

## 1. Flipkart GRiD (Campus Tech Challenge)
- **Role Offered**: SDE-1 (Full-Time) & Product Security Engineer
- **CTC Compensation**: ₹26 to ₹32 LPA CTC
- **Format**: Level 1 (Online Domain Quiz) → Level 2 (PoC Architecture & Prototype) → Level 3 (Grand Finale Bengaluru Sprint)
- **Key Success Factors**:
  - Optimize microservices for high concurrent traffic (simulated Big Billion Days traffic).
  - Use Redis caching, asynchronous queueing with Apache Kafka, and Dockerized setups.
  - Defend system architecture with Locust benchmark performance metrics.

---

## 2. TCS CodeVita (The Global Coding Contest)
- **Offers Unlocked**:
  - **TCS Innovator**: ₹11 LPA CTC (Top Rankers)
  - **TCS Digital**: ₹7.5 LPA CTC (High percentile in Round 1/2)
  - **TCS Ninja**: ₹3.36 LPA CTC
- **Format**: 6-hour continuous online competitive programming sprint.
- **Key Success Factors**:
  - Master Dynamic Programming, Graph Algorithms (Dijkstra, BFS/DFS), and Combinatorics.
  - Zero tolerance for plagiarism; MOSS heuristic analysis scans all codebases.

---

## 3. Smart India Hackathon (SIH)
- **Sponsors**: Ministry of Education Innovation Cell, AICTE, Central Ministries, and PSUs.
- **Prizes**: ₹1,00,000 INR cash prize per problem statement.
- **Format**: College Internal Round nomination → Power presentation evaluation → 36-hour non-stop Grand Finale.
- **Key Success Factors**:
  - Multi-disciplinary team of 6 (mandatory at least 1 female teammate).
  - Solve practical administrative bottlenecks with clean deployment (database, authentication, responsive web/mobile frontend).

---

## 4. Amazon ML Challenge (India)
- **Tracks**: Multimodal deep learning, optical character recognition, e-commerce product extraction.
- **Offers**: Direct Amazon India ML Engineer / Applied Scientist interview loops.
- **Key Success Factors**:
  - Metric-driven cross-validation (Macro F1, NDCG).
  - Pair text transformers (BERT/RoBERTa) with vision backbones (ResNet/ViT).
