1. Executive Overview & Value Proposition

The Career Trajectory Platform is a deterministic, client-side intelligence engine that charts career progressions for both engineering students (Campus-to-Dream) and working professionals (Lateral / Promotion Simulator) across 56 leading Indian employers. Unlike generic career sites that provide vague or US-centric advice, HireFlow models the strict realities of the Indian tech ecosystem: 1. Tier-1 vs. Tier-3 College Reality: Models on-campus placement tiers (Super-Dream, Dream, Mass/Differential), eligibility cutoffs (CGPA, active backlogs, gap years), circuit vs. non-circuit branch restrictions, and off-campus contest pathways. 2. Deterministic Promotion Cadences: Real review cycles (annual vs. bi-annual), calibration committee layers (manager -> director -> cross-functional promo panel), and level-specific stall rates (e.g., 75% stall rate at Senior to Staff). 3. Transparent INR Compensation Modeling: Detailed splits between Base Salary, Target Performance Bonus, Joining/Sign-on Bonuses, and ESOPs / RSUs (Stocks) with standard 4-year vesting schedules. 4. 3. The Three Trajectory Operating Modes

Mode A: Campus-to-Dream Placement Pathway (Students & Freshers)

Designed specifically for Indian engineering students preparing for on-campus and off-campus placements. #### Candidate Inputs: - College Tier: - Tier 1: IITs, NITs, BITS, IIITs (Top Day-1 recruiters, Super-Dream drives). - Tier 2: Premier state universities, VIT, MIT Manipal, Thapar, RVCE (Product & high-growth GCC intake). - Tier 3: Regional affiliated colleges (Mass recruiters, differential hiring tracks, off-campus contest focus). - Degree & Branch: - B.Tech, M.Tech, MCA, Dual Degree across Software, Electronics, Electrical, Mechanical, Civil, Chemical, etc. - Current Academic Standing: - Graduation Year & Semester (calculated via [src/lib/academicCalendar.ts](file:///c:/Users/yadav/.gemini/antigravity-ide/scratch/hireflow/src/lib/academicCalendar.ts)). - B.Tech CGPA (10-point scale). - 10th & 12th Class percentages. - Active and dead backlog history. - Academic gap years (pre-college or during college). - Target Company & Role: Desired dream organization (e.g., Google India, Microsoft, Flipkart, D. E. Shaw). #### Engine Outputs: 1. Direct Eligibility Status: Evaluates the candidate against official criteria (ELIGIBLE, CONDITIONAL, or INELIGIBLE) with precise diagnostic violation pills. 2. Selection Process Roadmap: Full stage-by-stage hiring funnel: - Round 1 (Online Assessment / OA): Platform (HackerEarth, Mettl, HackerRank), test duration in minutes, topic distribution (DSA, Aptitude, Core CS), passing cutoff criteria, and elimination rules. - Round 2 (Technical Interview 1): Core data structures, algorithms, live coding, and Big-O optimization. - Round 3 (Technical Interview 2 / LLD): Object-oriented programming, schema design, database query optimization, system boundaries. - Round 4 (Managerial / Techno-Behavioral): Project deep dive, situational judgment, CS fundamental defense. - Round 5 (HR / Leadership Fit): Cultural alignment, communication, relocation readiness. 3. Training & Service Agreements: - Mandatory service bonds (duration in months, exit penalty in INR). - Internship stipend vs. full-time converted CTC. 4. Bridge Pathway (For Tier-3 / Service Route Students): - If direct Tier-1 entry is blocked due to campus gatekeeping or branch restrictions, the engine automatically synthesizes an actionable Bridge Route: - Phase 1: Enter Tier-1 IT Services (TCS Digital, Infosys SP) or Tier-2 Product startup. - Phase 2: 18–24 months of production engineering, open-source portfolio development, and distributed systems mastery. - Phase 3: Lateral switch to Target Dream Company bypassing campus restrictions.

Mode B: Professional Promotion & Lateral Switch Simulator

Designed for working software engineers to evaluate intra-company promotions or strategic lateral company switches. #### Candidate Inputs: - Current Node: Company (e.g., Amazon India) and Level (e.g., SDE-2 / L5). - Target Node: Company (e.g., Google India) and Level (e.g., Staff SWE / L6). - Current Total CTC: Actual compensation in LPA (INR). - Experience: Total career experience in years & time spent in current level. - Performance Rating Bracket: - CONSISTENTLY_EXCEEDS: Top 5–10% talent, fast-track promo cycles (0.75x duration multiplier). - EXCEEDS: Strong consistent performer, typical timeline (1.00x duration multiplier). - MEETS: Standard performance, upper bound duration (1.30x duration multiplier). #### Engine Outputs: 1. Multi-Step Progression Plan: - Classifies the trajectory as an Internal Promotion, Lateral Switch, Downlevel Switch (common when transitioning from IT Services to Big Tech), or Multi-Hop Trajectory. 2. Timeline Forecasts: - Minimum, likely, and maximum years to reach the target level. 3. Promotion Process Governance: - Review cycle cadence (annual, semi-annual, quarterly) and calendar window. - Required evidence artifacts (Design Docs, RFCs, Postmortems, Mentorship logs, Business Impact metrics). - Review calibration committee layers (manager -> director -> cross-functional promo panel). 4. Compensation Trajectory (INR ₹): - Forecasted base, bonus, and stock grants across each year of the journey. - Percentage compensation jump on promotion or company switch. 5. Promotion Blockers & Stall Rates: - Level stall rates (e.g., 75% stall rate at Senior L5 -> Staff L6). - Pre-loaded obstacle checklist with action plans.

Mode C: Company Ladder & Level Explorer

An interactive reference catalog enabling users to inspect and compare engineering ladders side-by-side across companies. - Level-by-Level Breakdown: From Entry (L3) to Fellow/Distinguished (L8). - Normalized Industry Equivalence: Side-by-side mapping of equivalent ranks across Big Tech, Unicorns, GCCs, and IT Services. - Detailed Competency Expectations: Scope, Impact, and Influence definitions per level. - Compensation Bands: Real-world INR base salary and total compensation percentiles.

Mode D: HireFlow Suite Tier Positioning Bridge

Located in [src/features/suite/integration/trajectory.ts](file:///c:/Users/yadav/.gemini/antigravity-ide/scratch/hireflow/src/features/suite/integration/trajectory.ts), this engine connects candidate resumes to the Career Trajectory system: - Evaluates uploaded resume competencies against market tiers: - Tier C: Early Career & IT Services (TCS, Infosys, Wipro). - Tier B: Mid-Market & Growth Tech (Freshworks, Zoho, Thoughtworks). - Tier A: High-Scale Unicorns (Razorpay, Swiggy, CRED, PhonePe). - Tier S: Elite Product & Quant (Google, Meta, Uber, Tower Research, Graviton). - Computes Readiness Score (0-100) toward the next tier. - Maps specific Tier Gaps to practical Build Briefs (e.g., Distributed Rate Limiter, Financial Ledger).

6. Algorithmic Engines & Mathematical Formulas

6.1 Deterministic Path Traversal Algorithm

Located in [src/lib/careerEngine.ts](file:///c:/Users/yadav/.gemini/antigravity-ide/scratch/hireflow/src/lib/careerEngine.ts), resolvePath() resolves transitions deterministically: 1. Intra-Company Ladder Progression: - If source.companyId === target.companyId: - Collects all intermediate levels ordered by rank. - Synthesizes INTERNAL_PROMO steps with level-specific requirements and blockers. 2. Cross-Company Transition: - Maps sourceLevel to canonical EquivalenceOrder. - Computes target entry level based on external hiring discounts. - Generates a LATERAL_SWITCH step followed by subsequent INTERNAL_PROMO steps. 3. Cumulative Compensation Projection: - Projects salary progression year-over-year: $$\text{TotalComp}_{year} = \text{Base}_{year} + \text{Bonus}_{year} + \text{Stock}_{year}$$ - Adjusts for annual increment cycles (typically 8–12% in India product firms).

6.2 Cross-Tier Down-Leveling & Lateral Discount Rules

When transitioning between different industry segments, external hiring panels routinely discount titles: | Transition Source | Transition Target | Down-Leveling Adjustment | Rationale | |---|---|---|---| | IT Services Tier 1 | Big Tech India | -1 to -2 Levels | "Tech Lead" in services typically maps to SDE-2 (L4) in Big Tech due to system design and coding bars. | | IT Services Tier 1 | Product Unicorn | -1 Level | Title deflation on transition to product-first engineering. | | Product Unicorn | Big Tech India | 0 (Parity) or -1 Level | Senior SWE at high-scale unicorn usually enters as SDE-2 or Senior depending on distributed scale depth. | | Big Tech India | Big Tech India | 0 (Parity) | Peer equivalence respected across FAANG tier. | | GCC / Finance | Product Unicorn | 0 (Parity) | Direct mapping with base-heavy to equity-heavy rebalancing. |

6.3 Compensation Modeling & Split Multipliers

Indian tech compensation structures vary significantly by tier. HireFlow models exact splits: - Big Tech (Tier S): - Base: 45% – 55% - Target Bonus: 15% – 20% - Stock / RSUs (US Dollar denominated): 30% – 40% - Product Unicorns (Tier A): - Base: 60% – 75% - Performance Bonus: 10% – 15% - ESOPs: 15% – 25% - IT Services (Tier C): - Base: 80% – 85% - Variable Pay: 15% – 20% - Stocks: 0% (except senior executive band)

6.4 Performance Acceleration & Duration Adjustments

The duration of each promotion step is adjusted by candidate performance bracket: $$\text{StepDuration} = \text{TypicalYears} \times \text{Multiplier}$$ - CONSISTENTLY_EXCEEDS: $\text{Multiplier} = 0.75\text{x}$ (Fast-track) - EXCEEDS: $\text{Multiplier} = 1.00\text{x}$ (On-cycle standard) - MEETS: $\text{Multiplier} = 1.30\text{x}$ (Extended cycle)

6.5 Student Eligibility & Gap Diagnostics

Located in [src/careers-core/derive/derivedViews.ts](file:///c:/Users/yadav/.gemini/antigravity-ide/scratch/hireflow/src/careers-core/derive/derivedViews.ts), evaluateEligibility() enforces 6 sequential checks: 1. Degree Check: Checks if candidate degree matches eligibility.degrees. 2. Branch Family Check: Evaluates branch classification (software, electronics-electrical, mechanical, civil). 3. CGPA Cutoff: Compares candidate CGPA against minimum (e.g. 7.5 or 8.0). 4. 10th & 12th Percentage: Validates high-school cutoffs (standard 60% or 70% in Indian campus recruitment). 5. Backlog Rules: Rejects if active backlogs exceed maxActiveBacklogs (typically 0 allowed). 6. Academic Gap Years: Rejects if total educational gap years exceed maxGapYears (typically $\le 1$).
