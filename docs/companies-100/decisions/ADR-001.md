# Architecture Decision Record (ADR-001)

## Title
Additive Schema Changes, Data Integrity Guardrails & Provenance for the 100 Companies Expansion

## Status
Accepted

## Date
2026-10-25

## Context
HireFlow's TalentLens and Scoring Suite provide calibrated readiness analysis, company intelligence, and eligibility cutoffs for Indian engineering candidates. As part of expanding the employer catalog by up to 100 new verified enterprise employers (covering Quant, GCCs, Automotive/IoT, AI/Data, High-Growth D2C/EdTech, and Mid-Market IT Consulting), we require an authoritative schema extension that strictly prevents data fabrication (Rules H1–H12), upholds Zero-Egress local execution, and ensures backward compatibility with all 141 legacy employers.

---

## Decisions

### 1. Company Schema Additive Extensions
We extend the canonical `Company` interface with the following additive, optional properties:
- **`depth`** (`'deep' | 'standard' | 'basic'`, optional):
  - `'deep'`: Comprehensive profile with 3–5 calibrated roles, verified compensation signals, and detailed interview stages (at most 30 companies).
  - `'standard'`: Representative profile with 1–2 verified roles and baseline company intelligence.
  - `'basic'`: Profile-only company entry with verified corporate metadata, campus cutoffs, and official careers portal link (0 synthetic roles).
  - *Legacy default*: Unspecified / treated as `'deep'`.
- **`dataStatus`** (`'legacy_unaudited' | 'sourced' | 'editorial' | 'quarantined'`, optional):
  - `'legacy_unaudited'`: All existing 141 catalog employers not yet audited under H1–H12.
  - `'sourced'`: Direct factual extraction from official company pages with verified ledger entries.
  - `'editorial'`: Structural or archetype classification backed by expert editorial consensus.
  - `'quarantined'`: Employers lacking verifiable India early-career presence or official web sources (omitted from shipping builds).
- **`officialSiteUrl`** (`string`, optional): Authoritative root domain URL.
- **`careersUrl`** (`string`, optional): Direct link to corporate careers or campus recruitment portal.
- **`tierRationale`** (`string`, optional): One-line explanation of tier classification.
- **`sources`** (`Array<{ field: string; sourceKind: 'official' | 'company-published'; url: string; lastCheckedAt: string; checkedBy: string; }>`, optional):
  - Detailed field-level provenance records.
- **Loosening to Optional**:
  - `avgPackage`, `glassdoor`, `hiring2023`, `hiring2024`, `openRoles` are explicitly typed as optional (`?`). Unverified metrics are never imputed.

### 2. Role Schema Additive Extensions
We extend the canonical `Role` interface with additive, optional properties:
- **`profileKind`** (`'representative'`, optional):
  - Explicitly identifies roles generated from curated job family templates rather than scraping dynamic job postings.
- **`competencyFit`** (`'good' | 'approximate'`, optional):
  - Set to `'approximate'` when core technical skills in the domain (e.g., C/C++, Embedded C, RTOS, AUTOSAR, FPGA, low-latency) are not represented in the 20 engine competency keys.
- **`uncoveredSkills`** (`string[]`, optional):
  - List of essential skills required by the role that lie outside the 20 recognized competency keys.
- **`jobFamily`** (`'software-eng' | 'data-ai' | 'quant' | 'embedded-hardware' | 'platform-infra' | 'consulting-solutions' | 'other'`, optional).
- **Loosening to Optional**:
  - `compensation`, `benefits`, `hiringProcess`, `selectionProcess` are typed as optional (`?`).

### 3. Eligibility Schema: Strict Nullability (Rule H7)
In `CompanyEligibilityRule`:
- **`sourceUrl`** (`string`, optional): Direct URL to official source.
- **`sourceKind`** (`'official' | 'company-published'`, optional): Category of provenance.
- **`aggregatePercent`**: `number | null`
- **`cgpaEquivalent`**: `number | null`
- **Rule**: If an official page does not state criteria, no rule is manufactured. If a page states only CGPA or only percentage, the unmentioned metric is set to `null` with zero artificial conversion (Rule H7).

### 4. Legacy Migration & Backward Compatibility
- Existing 141 companies in `RAW_COMPANIES` remain untouched in source data (Rule W4).
- The `attachProvenance` migration helper tags existing companies as `dataStatus: 'legacy_unaudited'` and defaults missing fields safely.

---

## Consequences & Guarantees
- Zero breaking changes to existing 141 employers or test assertions.
- Safe rendering across all user-facing components without `undefined`, `NaN`, or broken layout states.
- Absolute transparency regarding source attribution, audit date, and editorial status.
