# Credential & Opportunity Engine — Comprehensive Catalog Guide

## Overview
The Credential & Opportunity Engine is a core subsection inside the HireFlow Design Suite designed to help BTech and undergraduate college students discover industry-recognized certifications, premier Indian hackathons, and paid open-source fellowships that generate verifiable resume evidence.

## Catalog Summary Statistics
- **Total Opportunities**: 42 Curated Opportunities
- **Certifications**: 21 Industry Vendor & Professional Certifications
- **Competitions & Hackathons**: 13 High-Yield Events (including 10 Indian Collegiate Flagships)
- **Open-Source Fellowships**: 8 Paid Programs & Research Fellowships
- **Pricing Standard**: Indian Rupee (INR / ₹) with global USD context
- **Pre-Placement Offers (PPOs)**: Up to ₹32 LPA CTC for winning/finalist teams
