1. OVERVIEW & INDUSTRY VALUE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Smart India Hackathon (SIH) is the world's largest open innovation model launched by the Ministry of Education (MoE), AICTE, and MIC. Over 50,000 collegiate teams compete on real-world challenges submitted by Central Ministries, State Governments, PSUs, and top industries.

2. TESTED DOMAINS & SYLLABUS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Full-stack software application development or IoT/embedded hardware prototyping
2. API security, encrypted database schemas, and role-based access control (RBAC)
3. Resilient architecture handling rural/offline network conditions and regional languages
4. Live 36-hour continuous sprint under technical jury evaluation from Ministry domain experts

3. STUDENT AID & FEE WAIVER
----------------------------------------------------------------------
MoE Innovation Cell reimburses train travel allowances and provides food/stay at Nodal Centers.
Step 1: Form a mandatory 6-member team with at least 1 female teammate.
Step 2: Pitch your solution at your College Internal Hackathon conducted by your college SPOC.
Step 3: College SPOC nominates top selected teams on the official sih.gov.in portal.
Step 4: Selected finalists travel to designated All-India Nodal Centers for a 36-hour non-stop Grand Finale.
