PROBLEM STATEMENT
----------------------------------------
Large document corpora (legal, medical, scientific) contain implicit relationships between entities that are difficult to query. Build a knowledge graph construction pipeline that automatically extracts named entities, relationships, and attributes from unstructured text, stores them in a graph database, and enables complex relational reasoning queries.

USE CASES IN THE REAL WORLD
----------------------------------------
Drug discovery (entity relationships between drugs, proteins, diseases), legal research (case law relationships), financial analysis (company executive relationships), and Wikipedia-style knowledge bases. Google Knowledge Graph and Bing's entity understanding are the largest implementations.
