PROBLEM STATEMENT
----------------------------------------
Fintech platforms process millions of transaction events across third-party gateways (Stripe, Razorpay, Bank APIs) that often mismatch due to webhook dropped packets, bank settlements, and timezone drift. Manual reconciliation teams cost millions of rupees annually. Build an automated reconciliation engine with idempotent ledger tracking.

----------------------------------------
Every payment company in India — Razorpay, PayU, BillDesk, Paytm — runs reconciliation systems. Banks, NBFCs, and lending platforms reconcile millions of transactions daily. This is a core infrastructure component for any company processing real money.

ARCHITECTURE & DATA FLOW
----------------------------------------
Kafka consumers ingest raw gateway webhooks into an append-only transaction ledger in PostgreSQL. A reconciliation worker runs interval matrix joins detecting pending, settled, and discrepant transactions, emitting webhook alerts on discrepancy anomalies.

SCALE & PERFORMANCE TARGETS
