ARCHITECTURE & DATA FLOW
----------------------------------------
Apache Airflow orchestrates training DAGs triggered on data drift or scheduled intervals. MLflow tracks experiments, parameters, and metrics. Docker images of trained models are pushed to a registry and deployed to Kubernetes with Seldon Core. Evidently AI monitors production prediction drift.

SCALE & PERFORMANCE TARGETS
----------------------------------------
Automated retraining triggered when data drift > 5%, new model deployed to 10% traffic within 30 minutes, full rollback capability in < 5 minutes.

3. A/B deployment splitting 10%/90% traffic between model versions via Kubernetes
4. Drift detection alert triggering retraining when feature distribution shifts beyond threshold
5. Full rollback test: deploy broken model, detect failure, revert to previous version
