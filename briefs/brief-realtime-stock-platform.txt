PROBLEM STATEMENT
----------------------------------------
Retail investors miss trading opportunities because they lack real-time price alerts and portfolio monitoring. Build a scalable stock market data platform that ingests live tick data, runs configurable alert rules, and pushes real-time notifications to a trading dashboard.

----------------------------------------

ARCHITECTURE & DATA FLOW
----------------------------------------
WebSocket data ingester consuming market tick feeds (NSE/BSE mock data). Kafka decouples ingestion from processing. TimescaleDB for tick-level time-series storage with OHLCV computation. Redis stores active alert rules and evaluates them in-memory. React dashboard receives pushes via Socket.IO.

SCALE & PERFORMANCE TARGETS
----------------------------------------
Process 50,000 tick events/sec, evaluate 10,000 active alert rules in < 20ms, and push real-time price updates to 1,000 concurrent WebSocket clients.
