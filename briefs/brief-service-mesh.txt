PROBLEM STATEMENT
----------------------------------------
Microservices architectures have insecure east-west (service-to-service) traffic by default, with no encryption, no service identity, and no traffic management policies. Build a service mesh implementation using Istio/Linkerd with mTLS, circuit breakers, traffic splitting, and distributed tracing.

USE CASES IN THE REAL WORLD
----------------------------------------
Large-scale microservices deployments at Flipkart, Paytm, and Ola use service meshes for zero-trust security and traffic management. Any organization with 20+ microservices needs a service mesh to manage complexity and enforce security policies consistently.

----------------------------------------

ARCHITECTURE & DATA FLOW
----------------------------------------
Istio control plane (Istiod) managing sidecar Envoy proxy injection into all pods. mTLS enforced for all east-west service communication. VirtualService for traffic splitting (90/10 canary). DestinationRule for circuit breaker configuration. Kiali for service mesh visualization and Jaeger for distributed tracing.

SCALE & PERFORMANCE TARGETS
----------------------------------------
Zero plaintext east-west traffic across 5+ microservices, 99.9% mTLS certificate rotation success rate, and < 5ms sidecar proxy overhead per request.
