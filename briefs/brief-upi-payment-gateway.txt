======================================================================

PROBLEM STATEMENT
----------------------------------------
India's UPI processes 12 billion transactions per month. The underlying payment routing, bank switching, settlement batching, and failed-transaction retry logic is enormously complex. Build a simplified UPI-style payment gateway with VPA (Virtual Payment Address) resolution, bank routing, and T+1 settlement batching.

USE CASES IN THE REAL WORLD
----------------------------------------
Directly applicable to roles at NPCI, Razorpay, PayU, PhonePe, and any company building payment infrastructure. Also relevant to neo-banking (Jupiter, Fi Money, Niyo) and BNPL (LazyPay, Simpl) companies building payment rails.

WHY THIS IS HARD (COMPETENCY REDUCER)
----------------------------------------
Payment gateway architecture is one of the most complex domains in software engineering — it requires understanding ACID transactions, idempotency, settlement accounting, bank switching logic, and regulatory compliance. This project positions you as a FinTech infrastructure specialist.

RECRUITER SHORTLIST SIGNAL

ARCHITECTURE & DATA FLOW
----------------------------------------
Spring Boot microservices: VPA Resolver (maps @phonepe addresses to bank account numbers), Payment Router (selects optimal bank rail based on success rate and latency), Transaction Ledger (PostgreSQL double-entry bookkeeping), Settlement Batcher (Kafka job aggregating T+1 settlements), Retry Engine (exponential backoff for failed transactions).

SCALE & PERFORMANCE TARGETS
----------------------------------------
Process 5,000 payment requests/sec with < 800ms end-to-end transaction time, 99.99% idempotency guarantee, and daily T+1 settlement batch completing in < 30 minutes.

SKILLS COVERED
----------------------------------------
Java, Spring Boot, PostgreSQL, Redis, Kafka, Microservices

SCREENER VERIFICATION RUBRIC
----------------------------------------
1. VPA resolution service mapping virtual addresses to bank routing info
2. Double-entry bookkeeping ledger preventing debit without matching credit
3. Idempotency key test: duplicate payment request returns same transaction ID, no double debit
4. Settlement batch job aggregating and netting positions across all merchants
5. Failure scenario tests: bank timeout, insufficient funds, invalid VPA handling
