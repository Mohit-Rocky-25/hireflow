PROBLEM STATEMENT

Interview preparation requires realistic practice with immediate feedback, but human mock interviews are expensive and scheduling-dependent. Build an AI interview platform that conducts role-appropriate technical/behavioral interviews, evaluates responses in real-time, and provides structured feedback with specific improvement suggestions.

ARCHITECTURE & DATA FLOW
----------------------------------------
React frontend with MediaRecorder capturing audio, streamed to backend via WebSocket. Whisper real-time transcription. GPT-4/Claude evaluating response against role-specific rubric using structured output. Follow-up question generation based on answer quality. Feedback report aggregated at session end.

SCALE & PERFORMANCE TARGETS
----------------------------------------
Process audio-to-text in < 3 seconds, evaluate and respond within 5 seconds of answer completion, and generate structured feedback report in < 10 seconds for a 30-minute session.
